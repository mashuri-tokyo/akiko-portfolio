About Me

I'm Akiko — a bilingual researcher and startup consultant based in Tokyo, working at the intersection of genomics, market intelligence, and global strategy.

My career began in academic research, where I was accepted as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National Institute of Genetics (NIG, Japan) — a foundation that continues to shape my data-driven approach to everything I do.

I then moved into industry, joining Genomelink, a Y Combinator-backed DNA startup, where I led genomic research, scientific content planning, and product development. Working from the U.S. office at Berkeley SkyDeck, I engaged daily with global founders, researchers, and investors in one of the world's most dynamic startup ecosystems.

Beyond research, I mentor PhD founders and deep-tech entrepreneurs through Beyond Next Ventures, The Alchemist Accelerator, and other programs — supporting commercialization, global communication, and market validation. I've collaborated with professionals from Japan, India, the U.S., New Zealand, the Netherlands, and Australia, and traveled to 33 countries.
